Hi!

I am Chaitanya Bashyam, a Ph.D. candidate in the Physics & Astronomy Department at Vanderbilt University! I am broadly interested in a subfield of physics called particle physics phenomenology, which bridges the gap between theory and experiment by asking how we can test models of new physics experimentally. Currently, my work is focused on combining the strengths of next-generation particle colliders and gravitational wave observatories to probe the microscopic properties of dark energy and learn about the universe's expansion history.

Other than physics, I am passionate about paleontology, Pokémon, and ancient history/philosophy. From time to time, I write about various topics which catch my attention. Moreover, I care deeply about pedagogy of science and public awareness of science. To this end, I aim to create educational Youtube videos on mainly scientific topics.
